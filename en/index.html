How it works

Three steps, once a year per person

  1. 1

    Add people

    Import from your contacts or add them by hand, with the dates that matter to them.

  2. 2

    Get reminded

    A few days ahead, not the morning of — so there's still time to think about what to say.

  3. 3

    Edit the draft, then send from WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.

Questions

What people usually ask

Does Smish send messages for me?

No. Smish prepares the text and opens WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS with the message ready. You read it and press send. Nothing leaves without you.

What happens to my contacts?

Smish does not upload your address book. It asks for access so you can pick who to add, and only the people you explicitly select reach the server. You can also add people by hand and never grant access at all.

Which languages?

The interface is in Hebrew and English. Greetings themselves are written in Hebrew or English, chosen per contact.

The greeting is written by AI. Doesn't that feel fake?

The draft is a starting point, not a finished product. Smish learns how you write and produces something close to it — then you edit. Most people change a line or two and send.
