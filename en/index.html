How it works

  1. 1

    Add people

    Import from your contacts or add them by hand, along with the dates that matter to them — a birthday, an anniversary, a holiday, or any personal occasion.

  2. 2

    Get reminded

    A few days ahead, not the morning of — so there's still time to think about what you want to say.

    Send it in your own voice

    Smish drafts a greeting in Hebrew or English, in the style you picked. Edit it, then send from WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.

Smish+

Smish is free to use, with a monthly allowance of generated greetings and a limit on how many people you can track. Smish+ lifts both. It's an auto-renewing subscription, billed through the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time from your store account.

Questions

Does Smish send messages for me?

No. Smish prepares the text and opens WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS with the message ready. You read it and press send. Nothing leaves without you.

What happens to my contacts?

Smish does not upload your address book. It asks for access so you can pick who to add, and only the people you explicitly select reach the server. You can also add people by hand and never grant access at all.

Which languages?

The interface is in Hebrew and English. Greetings themselves are written in Hebrew or English, chosen per contact.

Which occasions does it know about?

Birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and personal occasions you define yourself. This is not a birthday app — it runs all year.

The greeting is written by AI. Doesn't that feel fake?

The draft is a starting point, not a finished product. Smish learns how you write and produces something close to it — then you edit. Most people change a line or two and send.
