Smish

Stay in touch with the people who matter — without the guilt of forgetting.

Smish keeps track of the birthdays, anniversaries and holidays that matter to the people in your life, reminds you before they arrive, and helps you write something personal enough to actually be worth sending.

How it works

  • Add the people you care about and the occasions that matter to them.
  • Smish reminds you a few days ahead, not the morning of.
  • It drafts a greeting in your voice and language — you edit it, then send it from WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.

Smish+

Smish is free to use, with a monthly allowance of generated greetings and a limit on how many people you can track. Smish+ lifts both. It's an auto-renewing subscription, billed through the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time from your store account.
