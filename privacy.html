Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Smish is a mobile app that helps you write and send personal greetings to people you care about. This policy explains what it collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you get rid of it.

Smish is operated by Moriel Veksler ("we", "us"). Contact: moriel.veksler@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Account details — your email address and display name, received from Google or Apple when you sign in. To create and identify your account.
Contacts you choose to import — name, phone number, optional nickname, preferred greeting language, occasion dates such as birthdays and anniversaries, and any free-text notes you add about a person. To know who to remind you about and to personalise the greeting.
Greetings — the messages generated for you, your edits, and a record of which ones you marked as sent. To show your history and avoid repeating a greeting.
Preferences — theme, interface language, default greeting language, sender voice, message length, reminder timing and channel. To keep the app behaving the way you set it.
Usage counters — how many greetings you generated in the current month. To enforce the free tier's monthly allowance.
Subscription status — whether Smish+ is active, which product, when it expires, and which store it came from. To unlock paid features.

Only the contacts you pick. Smish asks for contacts access so you can choose people to import. It does not upload your address book. A contact reaches our servers only after you select them.

2. What we do not collect

  • Payment details. Card and billing information is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see it.
  • Location. Smish does not request or record your location.
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in Smish, and no third-party ad or analytics tracker.
  • Message contents from other apps. Smish cannot read your WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS conversations.

3. How greetings are generated

To write a greeting, Smish sends the relevant details for that one contact — their name or nickname, your relationship to them, the occasion, the language and tone you chose, and any notes you added about them — to an AI language model provider, which returns the text. Your full contact list is never sent, and the greeting is generated for you alone.

We use Google (Gemini) and/or Anthropic (Claude) as the model provider. These providers process the request on our behalf under their business terms and do not use this content to train their models.

5. Device permissions

  • Contacts — so you can pick people to import. Optional; you can add contacts by hand instead.
  • Calendar — to import birthdays you already keep in your calendar, and to write reminders back to it if you choose that reminder channel. Optional.
  • Notifications — to remind you about an upcoming occasion. Optional.

Declining any of these leaves the rest of the app working.

6. Sending messages

Smish does not send messages for you. When you send a greeting, Smish opens WhatsApp, Telegram, or your SMS app with the text prepared, and you press send. Whatever happens after that is governed by that app's own privacy policy, not this one.

7. How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting a contact removes that contact and its greetings. Deleting your account removes everything associated with it. Backups are purged on a rolling basis within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can, from inside the app:

  • Export a copy of your data.
  • Delete your account and all data attached to it — Settings → Delete account.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to correct your data, restrict how it is used, or object to processing. Email us and we will action it. If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are performance of a contract (running the service), legitimate interests (keeping it secure and working), and consent (device permissions).

9. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Sign-in uses short-lived access tokens; credentials are held in the device's secure storage. Access to the production database is restricted to the operator.

10. International transfers

Smish is available worldwide, and the providers above operate in several countries — including the United States. Where data leaves your region, it is transferred under the safeguards those providers offer, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Children

Smish is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will remove it.

12. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
